# Owner guide: managing your UK-used devices

Your developer gave you a login. Everything is done on one page.

## Open the admin page
- Go to **yourwebsite.com/#admin** (bookmark it on your phone and computer).
- Sign in with the email and password you were given.

## What you can do
- **Change a price:** type the new price in the Price box and press Enter (or tap away). It updates on the website straight away.
- **Add a photo:** tap the pencil on a device > **Choose photo** > pick one from your phone or computer > **Save device**.
  The photo is shrunk automatically, so big phone photos are fine.
- **Add a new device:** tap **Add device**, fill in brand, model, grade, price and specs, add a photo, tap **Save device**.
- **Hide a device (sold or out of stock):** tap the eye icon. It disappears from the website but stays in your list. Tap it again to bring it back.
- **Delete a device for good:** tap the red bin and confirm.
- **Search:** use the search box or the category menu at the top.

## Tips
- Leave the price empty to show "Ask for price" on the website.
- Specs: use the lines in the form (Name + Value), e.g. RAM / 16GB. Tap "Add a spec line" for more.
- Grade A = clean, little wear. Grade B = light scuffs.
- Use a clear, bright photo, landscape if possible.
- Always sign out on a shared computer.

## If something goes wrong
- "Wrong email or password": check for typos. Your developer can reset it.
- A change does not show on the website: refresh the page.
- Anything else: contact your developer.
